About Us

Values

High view of GOD

Our ultimate goal is to worship God in all of His majesty and holiness (Isaiah 6:1-4).

Sufficiency of SCRIPTURE

Our ultimate source of truth and life is the Bible - the very Word of God (2 Timothy 3:15-17).

GOSPEL-centered

All that we do, individually or corporately, is to be defined by the Gospel of Jesus Christ, thereby presenting the Lord Jesus to everyone. (1 Corinthians 2:2).
